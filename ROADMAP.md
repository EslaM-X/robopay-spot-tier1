# Roadmap

## North star

> Proof of authorship and provable engineering for the RoboPay Spot Tier-1
> submission: the repo's git history and release tags are the evidence.

## Sequenced next

1. **Keep the archive honest** — this repo is the original-authorship
   archive for the RoboPay bounty; any changes stay scoped to the 8 skills
   and their measured results.
2. **Reproducibility** — document the exact steps (muJoCo menagerie model
   + x402 payment gate) so anyone can re-run a paid skill episode.
3. **Distribution** — link the submission from the RoboPay bounty and from
   the flagship robot-sim repo, so the work is discoverable.

## Explicitly out of scope (for now)

- Expanding beyond the simulator scope (the profile is explicitly
  `simulator-only`).
- Forking or renaming the archive.
- Marketing copy that isn't backed by the measured skill table.
